# Product specification

Vela is an 18+ dating and friendship Mini App with original branding. Users sign in with Telegram, supply a self-declared birthdate, accept terms, upload moderated photos and discover compatible profiles. Mutual likes create a conversation. Users can end a connection, block, report, pause discovery and permanently delete their personal account data.

Eleven locales: en, zh, ru, hi, ur, fa, ar, tr, es, de, it. Chinese means Simplified; Pakistani means Urdu. Persian, Arabic and Urdu are RTL. No language is inferred from gender or ethnicity. Profile identity/gender is self-declared; no identity-verification claim is made.

Free male users may initiate three not-yet-started conversations per UTC day. A conversation already started by either participant can receive replies without consuming a start. Female and Other defaults are unlimited. All swipes are unlimited. Admin can change gender policy; product/legal review is required before deploying differential access in a market. Paid membership grants incoming-like access, unlimited starts and priority within each discovery page. Plans differ in duration and price, not feature set in this MVP.

Non-goals of this release: copying PURE branding/UI/assets, explicit content, automatic message translation, identity document verification, AI fake-account scoring, automated image moderation, infinite-scale discovery ranking, multiple API replicas, screenshot blocking, E2E encryption, automatic recurring billing and undo/rematch features.

Business prices are initial examples (100/300/750 Stars), editable by admin. This is not financial advice or an estimate of net revenue. Production seed initializes plans/policies only; legal content must be entered by the operator before launch.
